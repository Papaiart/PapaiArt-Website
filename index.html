One pipeline

From a sketch on a grid to a game you can ship

The game-development tools are designed to hand work to each other. Level Editor maps import into Level Modeller as real meshes. FPS Lab loads both, baked lighting included. Everything also exports to the engine you already use.

01

Level Editor

Sketch the layout. Draw sectors in 2D, walk them in 3D. The fastest way from idea to playable space.

.pamapQuake .mapEFPSEWAD in
02

Level Modeller

Carve & light it. Import the map as a mesh, carve it with booleans, texture every face and bake light on the GPU.

CSGlightmapsGLBOBJ
03

Terrain Generator

Grow the world. Eroded landscapes, skies and splat maps with presets for every major engine.

heightmapsFBXHDRIsplat
04

FPS Lab

Make it a game. Bring levels in with baked lighting, script the gameplay with nodes, build a standalone .exe.

Built from scratch

One developer, his own engines

PapaiArt is the software label of Bence Pápai and Babhár Kft. Every tool here runs on its own engine, written for real-time use. There are no heavyweight frameworks and no feature creep, just the features the job needs, built properly.

Performance firstInstant startup, low memory, responsive on modest hardware.
